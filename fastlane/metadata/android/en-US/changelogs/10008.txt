YuTbe v1.0.8

New
- Back 10 seconds and Forward 10 seconds buttons next to the previous and next buttons in the player.

Faster
- Faster app start: YuTbe no longer opens a second, hidden YouTube page at every launch (it was only needed for an old playback method). Links opened with YuTbe no longer wait up to 4 seconds before playing.
- Re-opening a video you watched in the last 30 minutes starts almost instantly, because its stream information is reused instead of fetched again.
- Pages load lighter: YouTube's tracking and ad requests are blocked before they leave the phone. Your YouTube watch history keeps working.

Lighter
- Less memory: at most 4 pages are kept open, and the video buffer is now 30 seconds instead of 60 (less RAM and less wasted data when skipping or closing a video).
- YuTbe's styles are added once per page instead of piling up.

Fixed
- Ads showing as grey boxes, YouTube's "recommendations don't fit?" prompts and a sometimes oversized bottom bar: YuTbe's styles and scripts could miss a page; they are now reliably applied again, and more ad and prompt types are hidden.
- Playing a playlist or the queue no longer stops with an error at a removed, private or blocked video; it is skipped with a short message.

Build
- The finished APK is only saved as dist/yutbe<version>.apk; the extra copy with a "-release" suffix is gone.
