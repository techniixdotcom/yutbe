Code clean-up: removed unused code and storage writes, leaner page scripts in background tabs.
Watch history in Extensions › Playback: every played video is listed, newest first; tap one to play it. Included in export and import.
About screen: "An advanced webview wrapper for YouTube. Based on Litube."
Much smaller cache: thumbnails are no longer stored for a year, the page cache is limited to 64 MB, the video cache to 128 MB and reuses data for replays, and downloads are no longer copied into the cache.
Option to hide the Subscriptions tab (Extensions › Interface).
All texts are translated into Spanish, French, Japanese, Korean, Russian, Turkish and Chinese.
With Show dislikes off, the dislike button is hidden completely.
Faster video loading: the extraction requests run in parallel, and the next video is prepared during the last 30 seconds.
Lower memory use: at most 6 pages are kept open at once.
Smaller APK: only the app's own languages and the processor types still in use are included.
Video pages no longer download the video twice: the page's own hidden player is kept from fetching video data, leaving all bandwidth to YuTbe's player.
In-app updates: YuTbe checks for a new release every time it starts, downloads it with a progress bar and opens the installer. Only a newer YuTbe signed with the same key is installed.
All new texts are translated.
