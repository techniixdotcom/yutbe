First YuTbe release.
Fixed playback stopping after about one minute (YouTube now blocks the old client; streams use the visionOS client).
Rejected streams are recovered automatically and playback continues at the same position.
The local queue no longer loops; when it ends YuTbe autoplays YouTube's suggestions, skipping recently watched videos.
Finished videos start from the beginning instead of the last seconds.
New setting: Autoplay suggested videos.
