Updated Gradle and AndroidX libraries.
New README.
The build checks that the APK carries the right version, keeps only the newest APK in dist and shows the version installed on the phone.

Separate start quality for Wi-Fi and mobile data (Extensions › Playback).
Sleep timer: pause after this video or at a set time (Extensions › Playback).
Videos leave the local queue once they have been played, whether they ran to the end or were skipped.
Queue list in Extensions › Playback: tap a video to remove it, or clear the whole queue.
Export and import settings, blocked channels, watch history and queue (Extensions › ⋮).
Pages now talk to the app through a channel only YouTube pages can use; embedded frames are shut out.
With Swipe down to minimize on, back, the bottom navigation and opening other pages all use the bottom bar.
The bottom navigation shows your account picture on "You" and opens the You page on the first tap.
Previous and next work in the full player, the mini player and the bottom bar.
Autoplay and the next button pick at random among the top suggested videos of the current video, so playback keeps going endlessly with similar videos when the queue is empty.
Background tabs no longer do page work, which keeps loading fast.
